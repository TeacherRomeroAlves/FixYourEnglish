How to play

  1. Choose a topic and click Start Game.
  2. Flip two cards. A correct pair stays visible but becomes dimmer.
  3. An incorrect pair flips back after a short pause.
  4. If you find the Joker, it flips back and exchanges places with a card from a pair you already found.
  5. Find all twelve pairs in the fewest attempts and shortest time.
