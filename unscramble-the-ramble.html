UNSCRAMBLE THE RAMBLE

Choose one grammar topic and start small. Turn a shared word bank into an affirmative sentence, a negative sentence, and a question. Three gentle steps, one topic at a time.

Your practice, your choice

What would you like to practice?

Each topic starts with very short sentences. Complete its three levels at your own pace.

Selecting a topic starts a fresh game with three checks per level.

Choosing another topic starts a new run. It does not carry over your current answers.

How to play

  1. Select a sentence row, then click words to add them. Or drag words into any row.
  2. Click a placed word to return it to the bank. Drag it before another word to reorder, or focus it and use the left/right arrow keys.
  3. Build one affirmative, one negative, and one question. Use every block; sentence lengths can vary.
  4. You have three checks per level. Feedback marks whole sentences only. Clearing the board does not restore checks.
  5. Get all three correct to unlock the next level. Finish Level 3 to win!

More than one answer can work. Stay with your chosen grammar topic. Contractions such as isn't and don't stay in a single block. The checker recognizes patterns for these word banks, not every possible English sentence. Capital letters and punctuation are handled for you.
