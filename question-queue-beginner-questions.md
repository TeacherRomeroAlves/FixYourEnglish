# Question Queue: Beginner Questions

**Partner:** Lucas, 20  
**Total:** 144 questions

## Verb to be

### About You

1. What is your name?
2. How old are you?
3. Are you tired today?
4. Where are you now?

### Family & Home

1. Who is your best friend?
2. Is your home big or small?
3. Are your neighbors friendly?
4. Tell me one room that is not quiet in your home.

### School & Work

1. Are you a student or a worker?
2. Who is your favorite teacher or coworker?
3. Is your school or workplace near your home?
4. Tell me something that is not difficult at school or work.

### Places

1. Where is your hometown?
2. Is there a park near your home?
3. What is your favorite place in your city?
4. Name a place that is not crowded on weekdays.

## Simple Present

### Home Routine

1. What time do you wake up?
2. What do you eat for breakfast?
3. Do you make your bed every day?
4. Tell me something you do not do in the morning.

### Work & School

1. Where do you work or study?
2. What time do your classes or work start?
3. Do you use English at work or school?
4. Tell me one thing you do not enjoy doing there.

### Free Time

1. What do you do in your free time?
2. Do you play any sports?
3. What kind of music do you like?
4. Tell me an activity you do not enjoy.

### Healthy Habits

1. How much water do you drink every day?
2. Do you eat vegetables often?
3. What time do you go to bed?
4. Tell me one unhealthy food you do not eat often.

### Abilities
