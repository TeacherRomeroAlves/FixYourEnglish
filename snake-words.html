How to play

  1. Click Start Game. The timer begins only then.
  2. Use the arrow keys, WASD, swipe, or the direction buttons.
  3. Eat the sentence words in order. Two choices are always on the board.
  4. A wrong word, wall, or body collision restarts the current level. The timer keeps running.
  5. Complete all seven levels and share your score and time.
