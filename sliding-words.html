A classic puzzle with an English twist

SLIDING WORDS

Eight words are trapped in a 3×3 grid. Slide one word at a time into the empty space until a complete English sentence appears.

8 word tiles 1 empty space 18 sentences No time limit

Sentence Puzzle

Slide every word into order

How to play

  1. Read the grid from left to right and top to bottom.
  2. Click a word beside the empty space to slide it. You can also drag that word into the gap.
  3. Only neighboring tiles can move. The finished puzzle has the empty space in the bottom-right corner.
  4. Arrange all eight words into one natural sentence. Punctuation stays attached to the final word.
