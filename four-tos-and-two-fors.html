4 TO'S AND 2 FOR'S

Practice the difference between to and for with short paragraphs. In each round, use exactly 4 TO blocks and 2 FOR blocks.

Pre-intermediate Intermediate 6 gaps 3 checks only

Grammar Game

Choose TO or FOR in context

Topic: Everyday life

How to play

  1. Read the paragraph and complete all 6 gaps.
  2. Click a block to send it to the next space, or drag it into place.
  3. Use exactly 4 TO blocks and 2 FOR blocks.
  4. You have 3 hearts. Every check uses one heart and powers the machine with your correct answers.
  5. Power all 6 parts of Machine 42 to advance automatically.
